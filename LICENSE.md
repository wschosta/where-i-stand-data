# Licenses for the data in this repository's releases

The release assets are the data files the Where I Stand app downloads. They are
compiled by Boilerplate LLC from public sources. **Different parts of the data are
under different terms**, and the terms of each source travel with its data.

## Wikipedia excerpts — CC BY-SA 4.0

`place-details.sqlite.gz` and `geometry.sqlite.gz` contain short excerpts from
English Wikipedia articles about U.S. places, written by Wikipedia's contributors.

- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- **Attribution:** each excerpt is stored with the title of the article it came
  from. The article, and its history of contributors, is at
  `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<title>`.
- **Changes:** the excerpts are shortened to their first sentences, and their
  spacing and punctuation are tidied. The wording is otherwise unchanged.
- **ShareAlike:** if you share these excerpts or an adaptation of them, you must
  do so under CC BY-SA 4.0 (or a compatible license), with the same attribution.

## City council and ward boundaries — each city's terms

`geometry.sqlite.gz` contains council or ward boundaries published by city
open-data portals. Each remains under the terms its city publishes it under.
Check the portal of the city concerned before reusing its boundaries.
